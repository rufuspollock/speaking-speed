title: Speaking Speed installs as a menu-bar app, and the rate now tracks your pace
promote: false
---

Speaking Speed now installs with one script as a normal Mac app that sits in the menu bar, starts listening on its own and can open at login, so there is no command to run before a call. The rate now counts your short pauses and was retuned on real recordings of read-aloud speech, so slowing down or pausing more visibly lowers the number (about 3.8 slow, 4.4 normal and 4.7 fast on the test clips).
