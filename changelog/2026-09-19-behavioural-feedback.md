title: A quiet dot and a nudge to pause, instead of a live number
promote: false
---

Version 2 of Speaking Speed stops showing your words per minute. The menu bar now shows a dot that only changes when there's something to do: after 15 seconds of talking without a pause, it turns orange and a small "pause" pill appears near the camera; if your pace stays fast for a while, it turns red with "slow down". After each conversation you get a notification asking how it felt (calm, OK or rushed), next to a one-line summary. Why we hid the number: [lessons from version 1](../docs/v1-lessons.md).
